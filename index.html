02 / PHILOSOPHY

The Art of the Unhurried Eye

“Photography is not about clicking endlessly. It is about patience, understanding the soul of the subject, and capturing life without artificial staging.”

In a digital world flooded with hyper-processed, AI-generated images, I search for the honest human spirit.

Every photograph I take in Bihar carries genuine atmosphere: the quiet contemplation of an elder with his morning paper, the innocent gaze of a child tucked in a winter blanket, the tired posture of a cycle-rickshaw puller resting on his handlebars, and the geometric shadows inside a passenger train.

I treat every frame with the discipline of silver gelatin printmaking: natural lighting, tonal richness, respectful distance, and uncompromising visual truth.
